# Passage — Bright Days Job Fair

For the pupil site and for teachers. British spelling. Line numbers must be shown on the left so feedback such as “(line 15)” works.

This is an online version of the source paper, not an original rewrite. Keep the wording below. Do not add the source paper’s handwritten date, the handwritten word “helper” above Cook’s Assistant, or the blue highlight on *calm down scared animals* (that highlight would hint at Question 3).

**Peter is at a job fair with his father. Read the job advertisements carefully, choose the best answers for Questions 1–5 and complete Question 6.**

## Bright Days Job Fair
### Find Your DREAM job here!

**(line 1)** Bright Days Job Fair
**(line 2)** Find Your DREAM job here!

**(line 3)** Writer for Kids’ Post
**(line 4)** Do you want to be a writer for our newspaper? Join us if you are good at
**(line 5)** English and familiar with what children aged 7 to 12 like. You need to:
**(line 6)** • be able to meet tight deadlines (we publish every day)
**(line 7)** • have lots of interesting ideas

**(line 8)** Part-time Photographer For Kids’ Weekly
**(line 9)** If you can take outstanding photos, e-mail us! You need to be fun-loving
**(line 10)** and creative. You will also need to love nature.
**(line 11)** If you get the job, you will work on Saturdays and Sundays.

**(line 12)** Vet’s Assistant
**(line 13)** (in Central, Mondays to Fridays)
**(line 14)** • Do you want to help our vet to take care of sick animals?
**(line 15)** • You need to be patient so that you can calm down scared animals.
**(line 16)** • If you love animals and are not afraid of big ones, this is the job for you.

**(line 17)** Cook’s Assistant at Rainbow Youth Centre
**(line 18)** Rainbow Youth Centre is hiring a cook’s assistant! If you become our cook’s
**(line 19)** assistant, you will need to come up with new ideas for kids’ menus. You just
**(line 20)** need to work a few hours a month on Sundays. There is no need to cook or
**(line 21)** carry heavy pots and pans.

**(line 22)** If you are interested, please tell us about yourself.
**(line 23)** E-mail us at: brightdays@joymail.com.
**(line 24)** For further details, please contact us. Tel.: 2646 2642.

- Left margin: line numbers, following the scheme above even if a wide layout reflows the sentences.
- No photograph is required. If a later decoration is added, it must not give an extra clue.
